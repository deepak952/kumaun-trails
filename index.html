How We Run Treks

Three things we don't compromise on.

Local Guides

Guides who grew up on these ridgelines

Every lead guide is from a village along the route, not flown in from elsewhere — they know the weather patterns, the river crossings, and the villagers who'll take you in if a plan changes.

Small Groups

Capped group sizes, every trek

Smaller groups move at a safer pace on exposed sections and leave a lighter footprint on trails that don't see much traffic to begin with.

Route Safety

Permits, weather calls, and a turn-back rule

Inner Line Permits handled before you arrive, a daily weather call at basecamp, and a standing rule that any guide can turn a group back — no exceptions for a tight schedule.

About

Started in Kumaun. Headed further out.

Kumaun Trails began with the routes around Bageshwar and Pithoragarh — Pindari, Kafni, Sunderdhunga, Milam. That's still where our documentation is deepest, and where we're starting. The plan is to extend the same route-by-route approach across North and Northeast India, rather than list a region before we actually know its trails.
